LIMITS OF THESE CHECKS

Stata is unavailable in the build environment. The do-files were statically
reviewed, but the Stata regressions, table rendering and graph rendering have NOT
been executed here. The independent numerical checks do not replace a Stata run.
No live WRDS or Gemini calls were made. Each user's licensed WRDS download may
reflect later vendor revisions. Fresh model availability/latency and new replies
cannot be guaranteed by the offline checks. Cost rates are explicit inherited
scenario assumptions; the missing lower-model price requires a documented quote.
